Trade-offs

Hallucination Rate ↔ Latency: We prioritize lower hallucination rates over latency because accurate, source-backed intelligence is more valuable than an instant but potentially misleading answer.
Latency ↔ Robustness: We prioritize robustness over latency because handling ambiguous and complex questions safely protects client trust, even if additional validation takes longer.

Insights

What I learned

Where the course was hardest, what you’ll keep doing, and the moment your thinking changed.

Friction

The hardest part was translating eval results into practical release decisions. I worked through this by prioritizing business impact, challenging my assumptions, and defining measurable thresholds and clear ownership.

Key learnings

I'll continue treating evals as a core part of AI product development, not just a final QA step. I'll define failure modes early, connect metrics to business risk, establish clear ship-or-hold criteria, and regularly reassess coverage as products evolve. Most importantly, I'll use eval evidence to support product decisions rather than relying on intuition alone. PS. loved the well-designed builers.

Aha moment

Catching 1 out of 1 critical failures looks like 100% success, but proves very little about real-world reliability.
